A statement whose proof is not in the development, stated as a claim: its
\texttt{sorry} is a warning, and a block or a check without the option
reports it as before.
\begin{leanclaim}[claim]{Claimed.result}{Claimed}
\end{leanclaim}
\begin{leanblock}[view=math]{Claimed.result}{Not a claim}
\end{leanblock}
The prose says it too \leancheck[claim]{Claimed.result}, and here without the
option \leancheck{Claimed.result}.
A claim with a sorried definition in its cone is still untrusted
\leancheck[claim]{Claimed.weak}.
A claim that is proved warns of nothing \leancheck[claim]{Claimed.proved}.
\end{document}
